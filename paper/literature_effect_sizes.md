## Notes for the expected-ecological-effect calculation (brief)
- Most estimates for PPIs, statins and metformin come from high-incidence East Asian claims databases and carry large heterogeneity or publication bias. No UK estimate against non-users was verified for these three groups.
- Jick 2006 (UK GPRD) is the only UK source for oral glucocorticoids. Population prevalence of current use is about 0.75–0.9% (van Staa 2000; Fardet 2011).
- Castellana 2019 reports an ICS population attributable fraction of 0.49% of TB cases, a direct benchmark for how small the ecological signal is likely to be.
- The Brassard ICS paper was published online in 2010 and in print in 2011 (vol 183). The Dixon BSRBR paper was published online in 2009 and in print in 2010.
